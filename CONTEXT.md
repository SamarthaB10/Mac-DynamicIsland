# Mac Island

Mac Island keeps music controls, files, and saved text near the MacBook notch.

## Language

**Hub file**:
A file or folder shown in the Hub. Removing its entry does not delete a local source.

**Online file**:
A temporary copy received from a browser drop. The Hub keeps it while a drag or upload uses it, then deletes it when the entry is removed.
_Avoid_: Drive download, which stays on disk.

**Accepted drop**:
An outgoing drag that another app accepted. Acceptance does not mean that the receiving app has finished copying the file.
